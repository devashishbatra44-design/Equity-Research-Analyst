## Overview

Netflix, Inc. is "one of the world’s leading entertainment services offering TV series, films, games and live programming across a wide variety of genres and languages" (10-k.txt:1718). The Company operates as "one operating segment" (10-k.txt:1721). Revenues are "primarily derived from monthly membership fees for services related to streaming content to our members" (10-k.txt:1721). The core strategy is to "grow our business globally within the parameters of our operating margin target" (10-k.txt:1719). The Company offers "a range of pricing plans, including our ad-supported subscription plan" (10-k.txt:1719).

For the year ended December 31, 2025, total revenues were $45,183,036 thousand (10-k.txt:2058). This represented a 16% increase compared to 2024 (10-k.txt:2058). Operating income was $13,326,603 thousand (10-k.txt:2060). The operating margin was 29.5% (10-k.txt:2061). Net income was $10,981,201 thousand (10-k.txt:2062). The Company discontinued the reporting of membership numbers, "focusing instead on revenue and operating margin as the primary financial metrics" (10-k.txt:2063).

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

The market for entertainment video is "intensely competitive and subject to rapid change" (10-k.txt:1725). Netflix competes with "linear television, streaming entertainment providers... video gaming providers, open content platform providers" (10-k.txt:1725). The Company also competes against "social media" (10-k.txt:1725). The objective is to "win moments of truth" (10-k.txt:1726).

Revenue growth in 2025 was "primarily due to the growth in memberships, price increases, and increased advertising revenue" (10-k.txt:2080). This growth was "partially offset by unfavorable changes in foreign exchange rates, net of hedging" (10-k.txt:2080). Streaming revenues by region for 2025 were:
*   United States and Canada: $19,957,152 thousand (10-k.txt:2087).
*   Europe, Middle East, and Africa: $14,514,646 thousand (10-k.txt:2088).
*   Latin America: $5,357,521 thousand (10-k.txt:2089).
*   Asia-Pacific: $5,353,717 thousand (10-k.txt:2090).

Operating margin increased by "approximately three percentage points" in 2025 (10-k.txt:2069). This was "primarily driven by the growth in revenues outpacing the growth in cost of revenues, sales and marketing, and general and administrative expenses" (10-k.txt:2069). Cost of revenues increased 11% in 2025 (10-k.txt:2113). This increase was "primarily due to a $1,121 million increase in content amortization" (10-k.txt:2115). It also included a "$1,116 million increase in other cost of revenues, primarily driven by non-income tax assessments in Brazil" (10-k.txt:2115). The Company does not expect these taxes to "materially impact our results of operations in future periods" (10-k.txt:2115).
