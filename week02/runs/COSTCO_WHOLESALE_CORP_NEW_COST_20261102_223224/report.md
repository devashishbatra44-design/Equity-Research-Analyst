# Overview

Costco operates membership warehouses and e-commerce sites. The company began operations in 1983. It operates in the U.S., Puerto Rico, Canada, Mexico, Japan, the U.K., Korea, Australia, Taiwan, China, Spain, France, Sweden, Iceland, and New Zealand. "We are principally engaged in the operation of membership warehouses in the United States (U.S.) and Puerto Rico, Canada, Mexico, Japan, the United Kingdom (U.K.), Korea, Australia, Taiwan, China, Spain, France, Sweden, Iceland, and New Zealand." (10-k.txt:721). The company operated 939 warehouses worldwide at August 30, 2026. "Costco operated 939, 914, and 890 warehouses worldwide at August 30, 2026, August 31, 2025, and September 1, 2024." (10-k.txt:721).

The business model relies on low prices and limited product selection. "We operate membership warehouses and e-commerce sites based on the concept that offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally-branded and private-label products in a wide range of categories will produce high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover." (10-k.txt:724). The company carries less than 4,000 active SKUs per warehouse. "We carry less than 4,000 active stock keeping units (SKUs) per warehouse in our core warehouse business, significantly less than other broadline retailers." (10-k.txt:731). The company operates on a 52/53-week fiscal year. "We report on a 52/53-week fiscal year, consisting of thirteen four-week periods and ending on the Sunday nearest the end of August." (10-k.txt:722).

Membership fees drive profitability. "The membership format is integral to our business and profitability." (10-k.txt:1018). The annual U.S. membership fee is $65. "Our annual fee for memberships is $65 in the U.S., varies in other countries and includes a free household card." (10-k.txt:760). Paid members totaled 84,100,000 at the end of 2026. "Paid members 1 | 84,100 | 81,000 | 76,200" (10-k.txt:763). Executive members represented 42,300,000 of total paid members in 2026. "Executive members represented 42,300, 38,700, and 35,400 of total paid members in 2026, 2025, and 2024." (10-k.txt:769). The worldwide renewal rate was 89.8% at the end of 2026. "Our member renewal rate was 92.3% in the U.S. and Canada and 89.8% worldwide at the end of 2026." (10-k.txt:770).

Fiscal year 2026 net sales increased 10% to $297,247 million. "Net sales increased 10% to $297,247, driven by an increase in comparable sales and sales at new warehouses;" (10-k.txt:1032). Net income increased 14% to $9,226 million. "Net income increased 14% to $9,226, $20.76 per diluted share, compared to $8,099, $18.21 per diluted share in 2025;" (10-k.txt:1044). Membership fee revenue increased 11% to $5,907 million. "Membership fee revenue increased 11% to $5,907, driven by new member sign-ups, membership fee increases, and upgrades to Executive membership;" (10-k.txt:1036).

# Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

Costco competes in a vigorous and widespread market. "Net sales growth and gross margins are also impacted by competition, which is vigorous and widespread, across a wide range of global, national, and regional wholesalers and retailers, including those with e-commerce operations." (10-k.txt:1010). The company believes it has adapted to economic and competitive changes. "While we cannot control or reliably predict general economic health or changes in competition, we believe that we have been successful, historically, in adapting our business to these changes, such as through adjustments to our pricing and merchandise mix, including increasing the penetration of our private-label items, and through online offerings." (10-k.txt:1010).

The company maintains a perception of "pricing authority." "We do not focus in the short-term on maximizing prices charged, but instead seek to maintain what we believe is a perception among our members of our “pricing authority” – consistently providing the most competitive values." (10-k.txt:1011). The company often sells inventory before paying for it. "We often sell inventory before we are required to pay for it, even while taking advantage of early payment discounts." (10-k.txt:724).

Growth is driven by comparable sales and new warehouse openings. "We believe that the most important driver of our profitability is increasing net sales, particularly comparable sales." (10-k.txt:1010). The company opened 28 new warehouses in 2026. "We opened 28 new warehouses, including three relocations, for a total of 25 net new warehouses: 18 in the U.S., five in our Canadian segment, and two in our Other International segment, compared to 27 new warehouses, including three relocations, in 2025;" (10-k.txt:1030). Square footage growth is becoming less substantial as sites become harder to secure. "As our warehouse base grows and available and desirable sites become more difficult to secure, square footage growth becomes a comparatively less substantial component of growth." (10-k.txt:1017).

Digitally-enabled sales represented 11% of total net sales in 2026. "Digitally-enabled sales, which represents sales that are initiated through a digital device, whether fulfilled through a warehouse or distribution center, as well as Costco Travel, represented 11% of total net sales in 2026." (10-k.txt:754). Digitally-enabled comparable sales grew 20.9% for the 52-week period. "Digitally-Enabled | 19.5% | 19.8% | 20.9% | 20.7%" (exhibit-99-1.txt:18). The gasoline business represented 11% of total net sales in 2026. "Our gasoline business represented 11% of total net sales in 2026." (10-k.txt:751).
