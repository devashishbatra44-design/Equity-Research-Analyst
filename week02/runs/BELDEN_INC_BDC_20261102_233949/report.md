## Overview

Belden Inc. is a "leading global supplier of complete connection solutions" (10-k.txt:2160). The company operates two reportable segments: Smart Infrastructure Solutions and Automation Solutions (10-k.txt:2160). Smart Infrastructure Solutions provides network infrastructure and broadband solutions (10-k.txt:2167). Automation Solutions provides digitization and automation solutions for IT/OT convergence (10-k.txt:2170). The company sells to distributors, end-users, installers, and original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) (10-k.txt:2160).
