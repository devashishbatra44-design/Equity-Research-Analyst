## Overview

Costco began operations in 1983. It operates membership warehouses in the U.S. and Puerto Rico and 13 other countries. It operated 939 warehouses at August 30, 2026. It also operated e-commerce sites in nine countries. Its common stock trades under “COST.” The filing states: “Costco operated 939, 914, and 890 warehouses worldwide at August 30, 2026, August 31, 2025, and September 1, 2024.” (10-k.txt:721)

The business model relies on low prices, a limited selection, and rapid inventory turnover. The filing states that this model enables Costco to operate at “significantly lower gross margins (net sales less merchandise costs) than most other retailers.” It also states: “We often sell inventory before we are required to pay for it.” (10-k.txt:724)

Costco buys most merchandise directly from suppliers and uses cross-docking depots or direct warehouse delivery. The filing states: “This process creates freight volume and handling efficiencies.” (10-k.txt:725–728) The average warehouse is approximately 148,000 square feet. (10-k.txt:729) Costco carries “less than 4,000” active warehouse SKUs and averages “from 9,000 to 11,000 SKUs online.” (10-k.txt:731)

Costco reports core merchandise, warehouse ancillary services, and other businesses. The filing lists gasoline, pharmacy, food court, optical, hearing aids, and tire installation as warehouse ancillary services. (10-k.txt:733–750) Costco operated 774 gas stations, and gasoline represented 11% of 2026 net sales. (10-k.txt:751) Digitally enabled sales represented 11% of 2026 net sales. (10-k.txt:754)

The U.S. annual membership fee is $65. (10-k.txt:760) Costco reported 84.1 million paid members and 150.4 million total cardholders for 2026. (10-k.txt:761–765) Executive members represented 42.3 million paid members and approximately 75.2% of worldwide net sales. (10-k.txt:769) The renewal rate was 92.3% in the U.S. and Canada and 89.8% worldwide. (10-k.txt:770)

## Market Positioning and Growth Outlook

Costco seeks to provide “a broad range of high-quality merchandise at prices we believe are consistently lower than elsewhere.” (10-k.txt:731) It does not focus on maximizing short-term prices. It seeks to maintain “pricing authority” by providing “the most competitive values.” (10-k.txt:1011)

Competition is “vigorous and widespread” across global, national, and regional wholesalers and retailers, including e-commerce operators. Costco states that it has adapted through pricing and merchandise-mix changes, higher private-label penetration, and online offerings. (10-k.txt:1010)

Net sales increased 10% to $297,247 million in 2026, driven by comparable sales and new warehouses. (10-k.txt:1032) Higher gasoline prices added $3,501 million, and currency changes added $1,332 million. (10-k.txt:1034) Membership fee revenue increased 11% to $5,907 million, driven by sign-ups, fee increases, and Executive upgrades. (10-k.txt:1036)

Gross margin percentage decreased three basis points. It increased 10 basis points excluding gasoline-price inflation. (10-k.txt:1038) SG&A as a percentage of net sales decreased 10 basis points and increased one basis point excluding gasoline-price inflation. (10-k.txt:1040) Net income increased 14% to $9,226 million, or $20.76 per diluted share. (10-k.txt:1044)

Costco opened 28 warehouses in 2026, including three relocations, for 25 net new warehouses. The openings included 18 in the U.S., five in Canada, and two in Other International. (10-k.txt:1030) Costco states that its square-footage growth rate is generally higher in many international markets because those markets have a smaller base. It expects that pattern to continue. (10-k.txt:1017)

## Future Developments and Strategic Recommendations

Costco planned to open up to 33 new warehouses, including five relocations, in 2027. The filing warns that “There can be no assurance that current expectations will be realized” and that plans may change. (10-k.txt:1152–1156) Costco also states: “We pursue diversification in our supply-chain and seek to expand in-country production.” (10-k.txt:755)

Trade actions have increased some merchandise costs. The filing states that exposure depends on “the type of goods, rates imposed, and timing of the tariffs and policy changes” and that higher tariffs could harm results. (10-k.txt:871) Costco may share cost increases with suppliers, pass increases to members, or hold prices steady. (10-k.txt:1011)

Executive-membership penetration and renewal rates materially influence profitability. Costco states that international membership growth and more online memberships reduce the worldwide renewal rate because online memberships renew “at a slightly lower rate on average.” (10-k.txt:1018)

Costco attributes warehouse productivity to employee commitment and efficiency. It targets at least 50% full-time employees. The 2026 U.S. and Canada retention rate was approximately 94% for employees with at least one year of tenure. (10-k.txt:784)

The cited filing passages provide warehouse-opening plans, supply-chain priorities, and membership drivers. They do not provide a specific fiscal 2027 revenue target. (10-k.txt:755, 1018, 1152–1156)
