# Human Investor Persona

Author: Devashish Batra

Frozen: November 21, 2026

I avoid assigning a positive multiple to negative owner cash flow. I also avoid relying on unsupported merger or liquidation valuations.

Conviction is "high" only if both the valuation and liquidity tests pass. Conviction is "low" if either test fails. It is "not computed" if a required input is unavailable.

I will change the less view only if the next results make the TTM positive owner cash flow, working capital of more than $0, and complete financing sufficent to cover at least 12 months of operating requirmemnt, with the recalculation low value per fully diluted share exceeding the market price.
