# Human Worksheet Configuration

Author: Devashish Batra

Frozen: November 21, 2026

Using the course owner-cash-flow formula (after-tax earnings plus depreciation and amortization minus capital expenditures), SUNation's FY 2025 owner cash flow is -$8.438312 million (-$10.892833 million + $2.503115 million - $0.048594 million). Because cash flow is negative, applying a positive earnings multiple is inappropriate.

Accordingly, both the low and mid valuations are set at $0 per share. This $0 figure serves as a conservative equity-value floor rather than a liquidation estimate. Reported book value is not used as a substitute due to ongoing capital requirements, intangible assets on the balance sheet, and unresolved merger, financing, and dilution risks.

The intended purchase size remains $500,000, subject to the rule that an order cannot exceed 5% of the 30-session median daily dollar volume. Under the evaluation framework, conviction is rated "high" only if both the valuation and liquidity tests pass, "low" if either test fails, and "not computed" if a required input is unavailable.

As of November 20, 2026, SUNation's official closing price was $1.71 per share, placing the $0 low valuation $1.71 below the market price. Because the supported low value falls below the official close, the resulting release view is "less."

Furthermore, with a 30-session median daily dollar volume of $108,724.220, a $500,000 purchase represents approximately 459.88% of median daily volume—well above the 5% threshold. Consequently, the liquidity test fails, resulting in a "low" conviction rating.

Ultimately, this methodology avoids assigning a positive multiple to negative owner cash flow while steering clear of unsupported merger or liquidation valuations.
