## Required readings and media

The complete list, including optional further reading, is on the [course readings page](https://cinderzhang.github.io/FIN67110-Fall2026/reference/readings.html).

### Week 1

- [Francis and Zhang (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.frl.2026.110127): introduction, Section 3.1, and Section 4. Purdue Libraries access may be required.
- [Anthropic, Building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents): through the first workflow section. The durable distinction is that code fixes a workflow path, while a model dynamically directs an agent's path and tools.
- [Karpathy, Software Is Changing (Again)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LCEmiRjPEtQ): prompt as program and the autonomy slider.
- [Zhang, The Middle Is for the Machine. The Edges Are for You](https://cinderzhang.substack.com/p/the-middle-is-for-the-machine-the): the DRIVER course method.
- [Karpathy post on readable model output](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2105819303471976479): use prose about 80% of the way to ASD-STE100.
- [ASD-STE100](https://www.asd-ste100.org/): controlled English with short, direct, unambiguous instructions.

### Week 2

- Francis and Zhang, Section 3.1 and Figure 2.
- The course report audit.
- [Kim, Muhn, and Nikolaev (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.17866): abstract and first results table. The course page records 60.35% accuracy for next-year earnings-direction predictions, about 7 percentage points above analysts in that study.
- [Anthropic financial-services agents](https://github.com/anthropics/financial-services): its current README says outputs are staged for human sign-off and that users must verify outputs. This supports the course's human-audit requirement.

### Weeks 3–8 index

- Week 3: course data page; LSEG and WRDS resources; `ai-hedge-fund` README; optional verifier research.
- Week 4: Graham, Buffett, and Marks are required. The other value-investing and AI-capex sources are further reading unless the new week page says otherwise.
- Week 5: a mutual fund summary prospectus, Sharpe's active-management arithmetic, the BIS 2026 annual report chapter, and Wang, Zhang, and Zhang (2020).
- Week 6: OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications for 2026 and the agent/tool section of Anthropic's article.
- Week 7: ASD-STE100, the writing skill, Karpathy's post, and Zhang's course-method essay.
- Week 8: no new reading.

Recheck each published week page before treating an item as required. The readings page may list both required and further readings.
