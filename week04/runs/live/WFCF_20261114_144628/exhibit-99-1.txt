CASTLE
ROCK, Colo., Nov. 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Where Food Comes From, Inc. (WFCF) (Nasdaq: WFCF), the most trusted resource for
independent, third-party verification of food production practices in North America, today announced financial results for its third
quarter and nine-month period ended September 30, 2026.
“We
are pleased to report strong third quarter financial results highlighted by 6% growth in our core verification and certification business
that is benefitting from resurgent demand for beef-related services and growing momentum in other service offerings,” said John
Saunders, Chairman and CEO. “Our profit metrics also showed continued strength with gross margins rising by 380 basis points to
42.1% and operating income growing 68% to $1.0 million from $600,000 a year ago – both reflecting the positive impact of our ongoing
cost down initiatives in conjunction with accelerating revenue.
“Our
ability to generate these results in the face of continued headwinds in our beef-related business underscores the importance of our diverse
and growing services portfolio. Although smaller cattle herd sizes have impacted product sales and certain verification services, newly
developed service offerings such as CARE and RaiseWell® Certified are growing rapidly and more than compensating. And, despite record
high beef prices, cattle producers are still capturing $100-per-head premiums when they use our value-add services. Additionally, we
are adding hundreds of new customers for our Organic, non-GMO, Gluten Free and Upcycled certifications, and we are benefitting from our
unique ability to offer service bundling that reduces costs and audit fatigue for our customers. With market research indicating that
consumer interest in sustainability, humane handling, and overall transparency in food raising practices has never been higher, our comprehensive
portfolio of services ideally positions us to continue leading our industry for years to come.”
Third
Quarter Results – 2026 vs. 2025
Total
revenue in the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, increased 4% to $7.3 million from $7.0 million.
● | Verification and certification services increased 6% to $5.9 million from $5.6 million.
● | Product revenue was flat at $1.2 million.
● | Professional services declined to $218,000 from $257,000.
Gross
margins in the third quarter improved by 380 basis points to 42.1% from 38.3% in the same quarter last year, reflecting operational efficiencies
across the business.
Selling,
general and administrative expense was flat at $2.1 million, reflecting both improved efficiencies and management’s commitment
to managing overhead costs.
Operating
income increased 68% to $1.0 million from $0.6 million due to the impact of cost-down initiatives relative to increasing revenue.
Net
income in the third quarter was $825,000, or $0.17 per diluted share, compared to net income of $1,146,000, or $0.22 per diluted share,
in the same quarter last year. The decline was primarily attributable to a $946,000 gain on sale of assets in the year-ago third quarter
related to the Company’s divestiture of its Progressive Beef interest.
The
Company bought back 21,170 shares of its common stock in the third quarter, equating to $268,000 in value returned to stockholders in
the period.
Nine-Month
Results – 2026 vs. 2025
Total
revenue for the nine-month period ended September 30, 2026, increased 2% to $19.3 million from $18.9 million in the same period last
year.
● | Verification and certification services increased 4% to $15.7 million from $15.1 million.
● | Product revenue was flat $2.9 million.
● | Professional services revenue declined to $731,000 from $912,000.
Gross
margins in the nine-month period increased to 40.5% from 38.9%, reflecting the year-to-date impact of cost reduction initiatives.
SG&A
expense decreased 3% year over year to $5.9 million from $6.1 million, reflecting management’s first quarter decision to return
approximately $400,000 in 2025 bonus compensation.
Income
from operations was $1.9 million, up 52% year over year from $1.3 million in the same period last year.
Net
income through nine months was $1.3 million, or $0.27 per diluted share, compared to net income of $1.7 million, or $0.34 per diluted
share, in the same period last year. The year-ago total of $1.7 million included a $946,000 gain on sale of the Progressive Beef interest.
The
Company generated $2.8 million in cash from operations year-to-date, up from $2.3 million in the comparable period last year.
Cash
and cash equivalents increased 39% at September 30 to $4.4 million from $3.2 million at December 31, 2025.
The
Company has bought back a total of 110,651 shares year-to-date in 2026. Over the past seven years, the Company has returned $17.5 million
in value to stockholders through buybacks and a special dividend.

About
Where Food Comes From, Inc.
Where Food Comes From, Inc. is America’s trusted resource for third party verification of
food production practices. Through proprietary technology and patented business processes, the Company estimates that it supports more
than 17,500 farmers, ranchers, vineyards, wineries, processors, retailers, distributors, trade associations, consumer brands and restaurants
with a wide variety of value-added services. Where Food Comes From solutions are used to verify food claims, optimize production practices
and enable food supply chains with analytics and data driven insights. In addition, the Company’s Where Food Comes From® retail
and restaurant labeling program uses web-based customer education tools to connect consumers to the sources of the food they purchase,
increasing meaningful consumer engagement for our clients.
