# Overview

Where Food Comes From, Inc. (WFCF) operates as a third-party verification provider for food production practices in North America, supporting approximately 17,500 customers including farmers, ranchers, processors, and retailers (10-k.txt:553). The company’s primary business involves conducting on-site and desk audits to verify claims regarding livestock, aquaculture, crops, and other food products, supplemented by professional consulting services and a retail labeling program (10-k.txt:556). For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025, total revenues were $24,892,000, a decrease from $25,746,000 in 2024 (10-k.txt:1369). Verification and certification service revenue was $20,102,000 in 2025 compared with $20,552,000 in 2024 (10-k.txt:1366). Product sales were $3,616,000 in 2025 compared with $3,803,000 in 2024 (10-k.txt:1367). Management linked the revenue pressure primarily to supply-side dynamics in the cattle industry and smaller beef cow herd sizes (10-k.txt:1398). Gross margin for 2025 decreased to 38.8% from 41.9% in 2024, attributed to inflationary price increases from cattle ear tag manufacturers and competitive market conditions (10-k.txt:1431). Segment operating income declined to $1,206,000 in 2025 from $2,207,000 in 2024 (10-k.txt:1387).

In the third quarter of 2026, the company reported a reversal in trend, with total revenue increasing 4% to $7,326,000 from $7,015,000 in the same quarter of 2025 (exhibit-99-1.txt:158). Verification and certification service revenue grew 6% to $5,897,000 (exhibit-99-1.txt:155), while product sales remained flat at $1,211,000 (exhibit-99-1.txt:156). Gross margin improved by 380 basis points to 42.1% from 38.3%, reflecting operational efficiencies and cost-down initiatives (exhibit-99-1.txt:59). Operating income increased 68% to $966,000 from $575,000 (exhibit-99-1.txt:166). Management attributes this improvement to resurgent demand for beef-related services and growing momentum in new service offerings such as CARE and RaiseWell® Certified (exhibit-99-1.txt:37).
