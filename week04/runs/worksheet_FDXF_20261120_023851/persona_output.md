## Why the security may be cheap

The pro rata distribution of 80.1% of FDXF [F005; Form 8-K accession 0001104659-26-068519, Item 2.01] may place shares with FedEx holders who did not select a standalone LTL investment. Code-built owner cash flow of 1,089.572 USD millions for the fiscal year ended 2026-05-31 [C003] produces a mid-case value of 136.2873582388258839226150767 USD per diluted share [C005], above the licensed 2026-11-19 close of 126.58 USD per share [C007]. The supported favorable case is therefore a limited normalization case in which historical cash generation survives the separation and the market discounts near-term transition pressure.

## Why the security may not be cheap

FDXF used debt financing to pay approximately 4,100 USD millions to FedEx before the spin-off [F009; Form 8-K accession 0001104659-26-068519, Item 8.01]. The approved 14.00x low case is 101.7612274849899933288859239 USD per diluted share [C004], which is below the licensed 2026-11-19 close of 126.58 USD per share [C007]. The total recurring incremental standalone cost remains not known [F018], while post-spin operating income decreased 6% for the four months ended 2026-09-30 [F042; Form 10-Q accession 0001628280-26-068246]. These facts indicate that the apparent discount to the mid case may compensate for leverage, margin pressure, and an unresolved standalone cost base.
